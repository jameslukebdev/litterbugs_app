Earn real money by reporting litter or cleaning it up with Litterbugs. Help clean up your community and get paid.

Report litter and earn real money

Take clear photos of litter you find, mark its location and publish a report. When a funded cleanup from your report is completed and approved, you get paid. You can earn money even when someone else does the cleanup.

Earn real money cleaning up

Explore the map, review the litter photos and check how much you can earn before claiming a cleanup. Complete the work and submit after photos. Once the cleanup is approved, your money is sent through Stripe.

Get paid for both

Report a spot and complete its cleanup to earn money for both. The money you earn for reporting is paid separately and does not reduce the cleaner's payment.

Find opportunities in your community

• Browse litter reports, photos, locations and available paid cleanups.
• Choose a cleanup that fits your ability and available time.
• Share your report so others can help fund the cleanup.
• Track reports and see before-and-after cleanup results.
• Contribute money to a cleanup you want to support.
