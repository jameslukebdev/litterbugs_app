Screenshot promotional eligibility calls for at least four 1080 × 1920 portrait or 1920 × 1080 landscape images demonstrating actual app experience: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9866151?hl=en

These are visual observations and published guidance, not claims about competitors' conversion rates.
