# Global Communities hub — local preview only

Owner correction: /communities is the global hub. City-specific reports, photos, organizations, results and guidance belong on each city page. No Communities work is authorized for production.

The local /communities page uses the existing Litterbugs header, footer, typography, colors, and existing licensed volunteer photography. It shows worldwide place search, intent choices for cleanups/groups/results, a generic participation overview, an organization-interest draft form with a service-area field, community FAQ, and clearly independent external cleanup networks. The initial rendered main contains no Boone, postal area, local report, organization or result content. City information is shown only after the visitor searches for that community and opens its page.

Research reviewed October 29, 2026:
- CleanupUK group and hub search: https://www.cleanupuk.org.uk/locations/ — location-first discovery and distinct group/hub roles.
- CleanupUK Community Cleanup Hubs: https://www.cleanupuk.org.uk/get-involved/hubs — equipment support is a specific capability, with availability and requirements, rather than a generic organization label.
- World Cleanup Day: https://www.worldcleanupday.org/get-involved — separate participation and organization routes, global-to-local discovery.
- EARTHDAY.ORG Great Global Cleanup: https://www.earthday.org/campaign/cleanup/ — location search and organizer resources in a global hub.

These examples inform the design, not claims about Litterbugs activity or partners. No network statistics, events, or partnerships were imported. External links are labeled independently. Dedicated community pages are not automatically generated for arbitrary searches.
