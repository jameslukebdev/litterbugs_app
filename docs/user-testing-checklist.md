# Litterbugs user-testing checklist

Allow about 20 minutes. Use the supplied test build and test account. Tell us
what you expect to happen as you go; there are no wrong answers.

Please do not use a real payment card, publish a social post, claim a real
cleanup, or delete your personal account. Payment and deletion exercises need
the separate test setup supplied by the facilitator.

1. **Find a place that needs help.** Explore the map, zoom in and out, and use
   the filters. Open a report and explain what its status and reward mean.
2. **Compare two reports.** Look at one with a reward and one without funding.
   Tell us what you could do next on each.
3. **Start a litter report.** Add a harmless test photo and use the Other and
   Extra details fields. Check that you can see what you type. Save for later,
   leave the app, then return and find your draft. Stop before publishing.
4. **Try sharing.** Preview the share card and open the sharing choices. Check
   the photo, litter type, reward and text size. Cancel before sending or posting.
5. **Explore funding.** Explain the contribution, fee and total. Open checkout
   and cancel. Use only the separate sandbox if asked to test a payment failure.
6. **Find your account tools.** Locate your reports, cleanup history, sign-in
   methods, help and privacy information. Tell us anything that is hard to find.
7. **Recover from an interruption.** With the facilitator, briefly disconnect
   the network while browsing. Reconnect and check whether the app recovers
   without losing your place or draft.
8. **Check comfort and clarity.** Increase your phone's text size. Review the
   report, funding and settings screens. Tell us about clipped text, crowded
   buttons, confusing wording or unexpected movement.

After each task, record: completed alone / needed help / could not complete.
For a problem, include the phone model, app build, steps, what you expected,
what happened, and a screenshot with personal information hidden.

Finish with: What felt easiest? What felt confusing? What would stop you from
using Litterbugs again?

## Facilitator-only exercises

- On a disposable account, save report and cleanup drafts, then delete the
  account. Verify the account, uploaded photos and device drafts are removed;
  test another account still works. Never use the owner's personal account.
- In the verified Stripe sandbox, test a declined payment and retry, cancelling
  checkout, and losing the connection during confirmation. Confirm that a retry
  does not create a second contribution or misleading receipt.
- Use only disposable moderation accounts. Confirm an abuse report appears in
  the private review queue and cannot be read or resolved by ordinary members.
  Do not upload harmful material to the public map.
